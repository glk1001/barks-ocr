PETER SCHILLING JR.

Peter Schilling Jr. is the author of *The End of Baseball*, and *Mark Twain's Mississippi River*. He also writes about film and the arts for a variety of publications. He has been reading and studying Carl Barks' entire catalogue since he was a child.
